
# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The `wrangler-tui` community aims to provide a respectful, constructive environment for everyone who participates in project discussions, issues, pull requests, and other community spaces.

We welcome participation regardless of experience level, background, nationality, identity, or preferred development environment.

## Expected Behaviour

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful, professional, and constructive.
- Critique technical decisions without attacking individuals.
- Provide reproducible evidence when reporting bugs.
- Accept good-faith technical disagreement.
- Respect the project's documented scope and contribution process.
- Protect other people's privacy and avoid publishing confidential information.
- Give and receive feedback with an emphasis on improving the project.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions.
- Repeatedly submitting malicious code or intentionally deceptive reports.
- Retaliation against people who raise concerns in good faith.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying expectations, reviewing reported conduct issues, and taking appropriate action when necessary.

Possible actions include a warning, removal of inappropriate content, restrictions on participation, or exclusion from project spaces in serious cases.

Actions should be proportionate to the circumstances.

## Reporting Issues

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through an appropriate contact channel listed in the repository.

Do not use public issue trackers to disclose sensitive personal information.

## Scope

This policy applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces used for official project activities.

## Attribution

This policy is adapted from common open-source community conduct practices. It is not a verbatim copy of the Contributor Covenant.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review reports in good faith and determine an appropriate response based on the available information.
